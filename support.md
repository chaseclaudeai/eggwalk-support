## Frequently asked questions

### How do I hatch an egg?
Walk, run and drink water. Each egg family warms from its own habit:
- **Forest eggs** warm from walking (1,000 steps is 1 point).
- **Storm eggs** warm from running (2 points per kilometre).
- **Ocean eggs** warm from water (1 point per 250 ml, plus a bonus when you reach your goal).
- **Mixed eggs** count all three, with a bonus on days you do all of them.

Other habits still help a little. The Incubator screen shows your egg's family and exactly how it warms.

### My steps aren't showing up.
1. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then **Privacy → Apps → EggWalk**, and make sure Steps, Walking + Running Distance, Active Energy and Workouts are turned on.
2. Open EggWalk and pull down on the Home screen to refresh.
3. Make sure your iPhone or Apple Watch is recording steps in the Health app.

### What are Care Days?
A Care Day is a day you meet your companion's favourite habit (for example, reaching your step goal for a Forest creature). Care Days are how creatures grow from Baby to Juvenile, Adult and Ascended. Missing a day never sets you back. Your creature just waits for you.

### What do Shards and Lures do?
Shards come from daily quests and from duplicate hatches. You can spend them to choose which egg family comes next (Compass), to find a species you haven't discovered (Dex Lure), or to aim for an Epic or Legendary (Epic+ Lure). Shards can't be bought.

### I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?
Streaks include one automatic Rest Day each week, so a single missed day won't break them. Earned rewards are never taken away.

### Where is my data?
Your game progress is stored on your device only. See the [Privacy Policy](/eggwalk-support/privacy/) for details. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

### How do I change my step or water goal?
Tap the gear icon on the Home screen, then use the Step goal and Water goal controls.

### How do I restore a purchase?
Open Settings (the gear icon), then tap **Restore Purchases** under EggWalk Plus. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to iOS Settings, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**.

# EggWalk Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026
**Developer:** Chase James Widner
**Contact:** chaseclaudeai@gmail.com

EggWalk is a game where your daily walking, running and water intake help hatch and raise creatures. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and I don't collect it.

## What EggWalk reads from Apple Health
With your permission, EggWalk reads these items from Apple Health:
- Step count
- Walking and running distance
- Active energy burned
- Workouts (used to recognise runs)

EggWalk only reads this data. It never writes to Apple Health. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → EggWalk) or in iOS Settings.

## What EggWalk stores on your device
EggWalk saves your game progress on your device only: your eggs, creatures, collection, quests, streaks, settings, and the water you log inside the app. This information is not sent to me or to any server. If you delete EggWalk, this data is deleted with it. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple controls.

## What I don't do
- I don't collect your personal information, and EggWalk has no account or sign-in.
- I don't use advertising, and I don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
- I don't sell, rent or share your health data or any other data.
- I don't use Health data for advertising or marketing.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, EggWalk schedules reminders on your device (for example, when an egg is ready). These are local notifications and don't involve a server.

## Purchases
If you choose to buy an optional subscription or item, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those transactions.

## Children
EggWalk is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Not medical advice
EggWalk is a game and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give health advice.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, I will update the date above. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
